# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Project participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Good-faith
technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and
publication of another person's private information are not.

Examples of positive participation include:

- giving actionable, technically grounded feedback
- respecting different experience levels and communication styles
- keeping discussions focused on the project and its users
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Use GitHub's private reporting and abuse-reporting features for sensitive concerns. Do not publish
private information in an issue. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as the
circumstances allow.

This code of conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces
where someone is representing the project.
